Enhancement with AI

By the end, you can say: here's what got better, and here's what I wouldn't trust it to do.

The five moves

  1. Pick one real targetA workflow, lesson or learning objective from your own work, with a clear problem.
  2. Name the problem and the learning goalWhat gets better for the learner, not only more convenient for you?
  3. Use AI to improve it, and show your promptsFeedback, an agent or chatbot, or a workflow change.
  4. Before → after, side by side CenterpieceA mockup or a walkthrough. Label what changed.
  5. Be honest about the limitsAccuracy, bias, access, cost, over-reliance, loss of judgment. Add at least one mitigation.
