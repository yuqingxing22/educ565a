SYLLABUS
============================================================
Course description
This course examines how K-12 teachers plan, select, and evaluate technology for teaching and learning. Students study frameworks for technology integration (TPACK, SAMR, SECTIONS), Universal Design for Learning, technology-supported assessment, and emerging issues including artificial intelligence, privacy, and equity. The course project is a technology-integrated unit plan developed in stages and submitted in Week 8. Prerequisite: admission to the M.Ed. in Educational Technology or permission of the instructor.

Instructor: Dr. Elaine Whitcombe, Associate Clinical Professor of Educational Technology · ewhitcombe@hrumail.example
Office hours: Optional virtual office hours by appointment, Tuesdays 4:00-6:00 pm ET. Email to book a 15-minute slot. Hours are not recorded.
Response time: Within 48 hours on weekdays. Messages sent Friday evening through Sunday are answered Monday.
Graduate Teaching Assistant (discussion grading): Jordan Ellis · jellis@hrumail.example

Learning outcomes
- Explain how technology, pedagogy, and content knowledge interact in a teaching decision, using the TPACK framework.
- Evaluate the role a technology plays in a lesson using the SAMR model and the SECTIONS criteria.
- Apply Universal Design for Learning guidelines to remove barriers in a technology-supported activity.
- Design formative and summative assessments that make appropriate use of technology.
- Analyze issues of access, privacy, and responsible use, including the use of AI tools, in K-12 settings.
- Create and defend a technology-integrated unit plan aligned to the ISTE Standards for Educators.
